# Onboarding — reference study

Watched frame by frame on 60fps.design (onboarding, intro, splash, permissions, map filters) and Dribbble "grocery app onboarding".

## What the good ones do
| Reference | What happens | What we take |
|---|---|---|
| Aave — value prop (3D clock / lock / coin) | Screen is still; only one tactile 3D object moves (hands turn, object tilts). Two-line bold headline ("Your money. Your way."), one grey line, page dots, one pill CTA. | One hero object per step, everything else calm. Short two-line headlines. |
| Grok Bot (Family-style) | White canvas, product name centred, colourful characters drift around the edges, black full-width "Sign in". | Colour comes from the objects, not the UI: real product cut-outs (tomato, banana, milk, bread, atta) scattered around a centred headline on a neutral surface. |
| Everyday — photo strip | A row of rounded photo pills slides across; one grows into a tall stadium card carrying real stats, then settles. | Hero collage of real catalogue photos; one tile expands into a "product card" with real name, price and "about 10 min". |
| Zomato — intro cards | Central card springs in from the right, previous slides out left; soft blurred grid of food behind; underlined headline; dark CTA + one reassurance line under it. | Recommendation-card motion for the value props; reassurance line under the CTA ("We only use your location to show what we can deliver"). |
| Yazio — permission primer | Progress bar on top, two icons joined by a link glyph, bold headline, two ticked benefits, Continue → system prompt. | Location primer before the OS prompt: store pin ⟷ home pin, two ticked benefits, then the system dialog. |
| Map welcome (Arc-style) | Brand mark reveals the real map in a spotlight with live pins. | Location step shows the real map around the store with the delivery radius drawn in, the user's pin drops in, then "We deliver here". |
| Dribbble grocery set (EcoThrive etc.) | Big produce photography, "Over 1 million members", 3 slides + sign-up. | Avoid: illustrated people, stock gradients, fake social proof numbers. |

## Rules that come out of it
- Neutral surface; colour only from product photos and the violet CTA. No gradients, no glow.
- One moving thing per screen; motion is triggered by mount, drag or tap — never an idle loop.
- Headlines are two short lines; one line of copy; one primary action; a quiet reassurance line.
- Real data on screen (catalogue photos, prices, store ETA, delivery radius) — never invented numbers.
- Progress is visible (segmented bar), Skip is always available, and the last step is location → phone login.
